How to read this

A career is a row. One person playing five careers in a sitting writes five rows and one visit. Visits is the closer number to “people”, but it resets if someone returns later or reloads, so it is a floor, not a headcount.

Nothing is logged until someone interacts. Visitors who load the page and leave without touching it write no row at all, so none of these figures include idle loads or crawlers. That makes the totals conservative and the engagement figures honest.

Active time is time the page was actually visible, accumulated across backgrounding — not wall-clock from open to close.

Countries come from IP geolocation, which is a location, not a nationality: a VPN or a traveller lands in the wrong row. Anything at one or two visits is noise, and a visit whose location could not be resolved is left out of the country ring — so it can total slightly below the headline visit count.
